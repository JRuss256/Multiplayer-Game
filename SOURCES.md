## Design reference

The user asked for a haunted trivia party game in the broad genre of *Trivia Murder Party*. That game's use of a horror-game-show frame, trivia, consequences for incorrect answers, and an endgame race informed the high-level structure. This project has its own title, setting, copy, questions, visual design, and memory challenge. References: [Jackbox Games: *Trivia Murder Party*](https://www.jackboxgames.com/games/trivia-murder-party) and [Jackbox Games: *Trivia Murder Party 3*](https://www.jackboxgames.com/games/trivia-murder-party-3).

## Contest rules reviewed

The supplied Official Rules PDF says a valid entry needs a project title, cover image, description, and URL. It lists the entry window as September 22 through **October 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific**. This differs by one day from the challenge summary's October 31 mention; follow the Official Rules date. Eligibility requirements include being at least 18 (or age of majority), a legal resident of an eligible country, and having an active Handshake account associated with the entry email.
