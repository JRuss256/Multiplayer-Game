Project title: THE NIGHT SHIFT

Project cover image: cover.png

Project description:
A live haunted trivia party for 2–6 players. One host opens the hotel; friends join from phones or laptops with a five-character room code. A shared tutorial explains the rules before play. Get five rounds of recent general-knowledge trivia right, or face the 15-second Mirror Test: watch three marks, then repeat them in order to protect one of three hearts. In the exit run, change your answer until you lock it; the question resolves once all living players lock or the timer ends. First to three correct escapes. Optional original ambient music and sound effects are synthesized in the browser. No account or download needed. Each revealed fact links to its source.
